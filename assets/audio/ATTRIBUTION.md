## Current procedural audio

Forest, lakeshore, crypt, market, forge, alchemist, campfire, tavern, combat, and
dragon ambience are generated by `SoundManager` at runtime. No third-party samples
are used for those cues.

## Scene music

The following tracks are by **Eric Matyas** and were downloaded from Soundimage on
2026-07-22 under the Soundimage attribution license:

- **Our Mountain v003** — exploration and character creation
- **Magic Clock Shop** — taverns, shops, and safe havens
- **Fantasy Game Background** — standard and elite encounters
- **Bog Creatures on the Move** — boss encounters

Source page: https://soundimage.org/fantasywonder/
Creator site: https://soundimage.org/
Attribution terms: https://soundimage.org/attribution-info/

The WAV masters were downmixed to mono PCM16 at 22,050 Hz, peak-normalized to
approximately -3 dBFS, and given a short loop-seam blend using
`tools/prepare_audio.py`. No composition or arrangement changes were made.

Required in-game credit:

> Our Mountain v003; Magic Clock Shop; Fantasy Game Background; Bog Creatures on
> the Move — by Eric Matyas — www.soundimage.org
