Gameplay ambience
-----------------
Forest, lakeshore, crypt, market, forge, alchemist, campfire, tavern, combat,
and dragon ambience are generated procedurally by SoundManager at runtime.
They use a dedicated audio channel and crossfade when the current location or
encounter changes. No additional third-party audio assets are required.
